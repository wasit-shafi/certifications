# Preparing for the exam

- **START before you are READY!**
- Check for any Active Voucher/Coupons/Discounts on LinkedIn/Tech Community, Your Company's Udemy Team/Enterprise Plan, Peer/Friend.
- Use AI for doubts, further explanation, etc
- Check guidelines before giving the exam.
- Prefer an exam test centre over at home if the exam centre is nearby.
- The invigilator will review the whole room/desk properly.
- Backup Wi-Fi connection.
- Pass the exam in 1 week? You may need to look more/less videos to clear the exam.
- Core CS Subjects & Key Topics (OS, Networking, DBMS, Hardware, Memory, Storage, Distributed Systems & Concurrency, Security, Architecture & Design Patterns, Web Servers, Proxies, Docker, k8s, CICD, etc)
- Additional Resource: Reddit/Medium/Blogs, etc.
- Why AWS `CLF-C02` (GCP, Azure, Linux Foundation/CNCF, Alibaba, MongoDB, Claude, etc)?
- Random Topic Revision 1 day before the exam
- Can I pass the exam without creating an AWS Console account?
- **Foundational** vs **Associate** vs **Professional** vs **Specialty** Certifications - `AIF-C01` & `CLF-C02`, are almost useless for you?
- Don't Signup for AWS Console? Free 1 Year Trial, set up AWS Budgets.
- **100% Confident** about clearing the exam? Solve by Elimination.
- **Pro Tip: The Smarter Approach for passing the Associate level certifications directly.**

> All the best for your exam, follow for more such content
